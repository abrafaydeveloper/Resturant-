Dessert Tips & Tricks

Temperature Matters

For perfect cakes, ensure ingredients are at room temperature. Cold ingredients don't blend well.

Measure Precisely

Baking is a science. Use measuring cups/spoons for consistent results every time.

Timing is Key

Don't overmix batter and set timers. Overmixing develops gluten, making desserts tough.

Healthy Swaps

Replace sugar with honey/maple syrup, all-purpose flour with whole wheat, cream with yogurt.
