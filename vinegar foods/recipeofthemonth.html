Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl with Lemon-Tahini Dressing

Vegan Healthy Easy Seasonal

A vibrant, nutrient-packed bowl featuring protein-rich quinoa, roasted seasonal vegetables, and our signature lemon-tahini dressing. This recipe celebrates fresh, locally-sourced ingredients with bold Mediterranean flavors.

For the Bowl
For the Dressing
1
Cook the Quinoa

In a medium saucepan, bring vegetable broth to a boil. Add quinoa, reduce heat to low, cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes until liquid is absorbed. Fluff with a fork and let it cool.

2
Roast the Vegetables

Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C). Toss bell pepper, zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and red onion with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a baking sheet and roast for 20-25 minutes until tender and slightly caramelized.

3
Prepare the Dressing

In a small bowl, whisk together tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, olive oil, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Add water 1 tablespoon at a time until desired consistency is reached.

4
Assemble the Bowls

Divide cooked quinoa among bowls. Top with roasted vegetables, chickpeas, olives, and avocado slices. Drizzle generously with lemon-tahini dressing and garnish with fresh parsley.
